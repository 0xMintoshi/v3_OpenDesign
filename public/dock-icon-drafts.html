Dock icon drafts

Icon-only dock buttons live or die on the icons — tooltips are invisible on touch, so each must read on its own at 20px. Two to three hand-drawn candidates per action, shown at real size (20px) and 3× zoom on both light and dark backgrounds. Paired actions (arch states, stage nav) get a side-by-side tellability check below.

Icon design phase
01

Upper Edentulous

Upper arch silhouette as seen in the chart — curve opening downward with a gum-line. Reads as "upper jaw, no teeth," not a bare ⏜.

02

Lower Edentulous

True counterpart to Upper: curve opening upward. Must be instantly tellable apart from Upper at 20px by orientation and silhouette weight, not orientation alone.

Tellability check — Upper vs Lower, same candidate letter, at 20px

03

Restore Upper (active state of Upper Edentulous)

Same arch icon with teeth present — a filled tooth row inside the arch. The icon itself flips meaning on click, not just the tint.

04

Restore Lower (active state of Lower Edentulous)

Lower counterpart of Restore Upper — teeth row inside the upward arch.

Tellability check — Edentulous (off) vs Restore (active), same arch, at 20px

05

Stage 2 →

"Advance to treatment planning" — arrow-forward combined with a treatment cue (small tooth + plus, or arch→arrow composite). Not a bare arrow.

06

← Stage 1

Return cue — arrow-back combined with the baseline-chart cue, mirroring the Stage 2 icon's language so the pair reads as forward/back.

Tellability check — Stage 2 → vs ← Stage 1, same candidate letter, at 20px

07

Summary →

Document/list glyph (quote lines) with a forward cue — "export to summary."

08

Clear Plan

Eraser / sweep glyph. Avoid a trash-can — that reads as "delete patient data," which this isn't.
