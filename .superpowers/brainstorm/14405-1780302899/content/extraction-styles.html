Extraction Mark — Visual Direction

Which style feels right for the extraction treatment indicator?

A — Current (baseline)

Simple thin ×, fixed position. No crown awareness.

B — Ringed ×

Bold × inside a translucent red circle centred on the crown. Clear, contained, visually distinct.

C — Faded tooth + bold ×

Crown dims to suggest removal; thick × reinforces the intent. Communicates "this tooth is gone."

D — Pill-arm ×

Rounded rectangular arms, thicker weight. Feels bold and medical, less "sketch mark."
